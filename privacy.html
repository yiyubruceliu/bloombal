Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This is a general privacy policy template for the Bloombal pre-release. It should be reviewed by legal counsel and tailored to your final data-processing arrangements before public launch.

Bloombal ("we", "us", "our") provides a Two-Factor Knowledge Validation service for educational institutions. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Bloombal you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and contact details you or your institution provide, such as name, work email, and role.
  • Institutional data we process on your behalf, such as assignment submissions and validation results, under the direction of your institution.
  • Usage data such as log files, device and browser information, and interactions with the service, used to operate and improve the product.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and secure the Bloombal service.
  • To perform knowledge validation and return results to your institution.
  • To communicate with you about the service, including updates and support.
  • To improve product performance, reliability, and user experience.

3. Legal basis and institutional role

For institutional data, your educational institution is the data controller and Bloombal acts as a processor, handling data only on documented instructions. Where applicable, processing relies on legitimate interests, contract performance, or consent collected by your institution.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share information only with service providers who support our operations under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations, or where required by law.

5. Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal, accounting, or reporting obligations. Institutional data is retained and deleted according to our agreement with your institution.

6. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. Requests relating to institutional data should be directed to your institution; we will support institutions in responding.

8. International transfers

Where data is transferred across borders, we apply appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@bloombal.com.
