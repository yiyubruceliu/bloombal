Are your students performing misconduct with ChatGPT?

With AI tools like ChatGPT widely available, education institutions are struggling to maintain academic integrity. AI plagiarism detection tools alone are losing the battle and are unreliable, leaving educators overwhelmed and students unaccountable.

Detectors flag text. They cannot confirm a student understands the work.

Unlock integrity with Two-Factor Knowledge Validation

Students prove understanding through written and oral knowledge checks. Bloombal integrates with your university LMS to extract assignments and run those checks inside the workflow you already use.

  1. 1

    Written knowledge check

    Factor 1 — students demonstrate they understand the submitted work in writing, not only that the text looks original.

  2. 2

    Oral knowledge check

    Factor 2 — a spoken validation step confirms understanding in real time, modeled on two-factor authentication.

  3. 3

    Lives in your LMS

    Assignments flow from the institution’s LMS so integrity stays inside teaching practice.
