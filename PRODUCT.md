## Stack

## Users

Primary users are education institutions — educators and academic administrators who need to keep assessment honest while students have access to generative AI.

## Product Purpose

Bloombal (rename of Perspective Mind AI) provides Two-Factor Knowledge Validation so institutions can confirm students understand submitted work, not only that the text looks original.

Success is institutions adopting the product as a practical integrity layer that still lets AI be used as a learning partner.

## Positioning

A real-time knowledge-validation step modeled on two-factor authentication, integrated with the institution’s LMS — distinct from plagiarism or AI-detection scanners.

## Operating Context

Used by universities and applied-science schools alongside an LMS. Partner institutions shown on the existing landing page: NHL Stenden University of Applied Sciences, MODUL University Vienna, FH Wien University of Applied Sciences. Public marketing site plus a pre-release app trial.
